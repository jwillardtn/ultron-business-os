## Capture / design QA
- Capture at 1440px+ wide; crop tightly enough that spreadsheet text is legible.
- Use the same device frame/background treatment across both products.
- Never show personal/customer data.
- Remove test rows unless clearly labeled SAMPLE.
- Ensure every screenshot matches the actual downloadable workbook version.
- Do not display a feature in marketing unless it exists in the shipped file.
- Export marketplace images in platform-supported dimensions at high resolution.
- Recheck spelling, price, filenames, and package contents immediately before upload.

## Conversion hypothesis after launch
Measure actual visits and purchases before changing the offer:
- Low/no visits → distribution problem.
- Meaningful visits, no purchases → test hero/thumbnail and first 2 gallery frames before changing product scope.
- Purchases but support confusion → improve onboarding/Quick Start.
- Purchases with low refund/support burden → preserve offer and expand organic distribution.

No revenue claim is valid until verified from storefront transaction data.
