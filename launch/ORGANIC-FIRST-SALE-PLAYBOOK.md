# ULTRON Organic First-Sale Playbook

## Objective
Get the first verified sale for P001, then P002, without paid advertising.

## Publication gate
Do not start promotion until the product has:
- a verified public storefront URL;
- a working checkout;
- the correct downloadable customer files;
- final price and product description;
- a successful owner-side checkout/preview verification where the marketplace permits it without a purchase.

## P001 launch sequence
Product: Home Service Profit & Pricing OS
Launch test price: $29

### Day 0
1. Publish and verify public URL.
2. Check thumbnail, title, description, price, and file package.
3. Record baseline storefront views and sales.
4. Add the public URL to ULTRON launch state.

### Organic distribution
Use only owner-controlled or legitimately participatory channels. No spam, fake reviews, fake scarcity, or fabricated testimonials.

Content angles:
1. "The revenue number home-service owners watch too closely" — explain why revenue without job-level gross margin can hide weak pricing.
2. "A $1,000 job can still lose money" — walk through labor, materials, overhead, and target margin.
3. "Estimate vs. actual" — explain why closing the loop after each job improves future quotes.
4. "Stop losing quotes in texts" — show a simple lead/job pipeline workflow.
5. "The weekly 15-minute owner review" — revenue, gross margin, open quotes, and job-cost variance.

CTA: Link directly to the verified P001 storefront page. Avoid income promises.

## P002 launch sequence
Product: Plumbing Business Operations Kit
Launch test price: $24

Run after P001 is live and its first distribution pass is complete.

Content angles:
- labor + material + permit estimating;
- margin check before sending a quote;
- estimated vs. actual plumbing job costs;
- maintenance/recall follow-up;
- weekly plumbing owner dashboard.

## Measurement
Capture at minimum:
- storefront views;
- sales;
- gross revenue;
- conversion rate when the platform provides it;
- refunds when applicable;
- traffic source when available.

Decision rules after meaningful traffic exists:
- No/very low traffic -> distribution problem. Improve reach before changing the product.
- Traffic but no sales -> listing/offer problem. Improve hero image, title, proof/demo, description, and value clarity.
- Sales -> preserve the converting offer and expand the channel/content angle that produced the traffic.
- Refunds/support confusion -> product/onboarding problem. Fix the deliverable before scaling distribution.

Never claim a sale or revenue unless verified from the storefront/payment data.

## Current blocker
Publishing and storefront analytics require authenticated Gumroad/Etsy control. Repository access alone cannot publish a marketplace listing or verify checkout.

## Priority order
1. Publish + verify P001.
2. Organic P001 distribution + measurement.
3. Publish + verify P002.
4. Organic P002 distribution + measurement.
5. Optimize using real traffic/conversion data.
6. Only then consider Product 003 or paid acquisition.
