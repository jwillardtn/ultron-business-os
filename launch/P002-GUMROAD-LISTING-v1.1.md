## Full description

You know how to do the work. This toolkit helps you organize the numbers behind it.

The Plumbing Business Operations Kit brings estimating and day-to-day tracking into one editable Excel workbook. Enter your fully loaded labor cost, material costs, overhead allocation and target gross margin to see a suggested quote and margin check. Keep leads/jobs in a simple pipeline, record what completed work cost, and review the business in one dashboard.

### Included
- Pricing Setup: labor cost, material markup, overhead and target margin.
- Quote Builder: labor, materials, equipment, permits, other costs, base quote, target-margin price, final quote and override warning.
- Lead & Job Pipeline: opportunities, status and follow-up.
- Job Costing: quoted revenue versus recorded actual job costs.
- Maintenance Recall: repeat-service follow-up.
- Dashboard: open pipeline, quoted revenue, costs and margins.
- One-page Quick Start PDF.
