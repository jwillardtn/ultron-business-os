## Description
Stop guessing what a job should cost. When labor, material costs, estimates and follow-ups live in different places, it is easy to miss a cost or lose track of an opportunity. Home Service Profit & Pricing OS puts the core numbers into one editable Excel workbook.

**Inside the download:** Eight-tab Excel workbook with pricing setup, material markup, a one-job quote calculator, optional override and below-target margin warning, 100-row job-costing tracker, 100-row lead pipeline, completed-job revenue/gross-profit dashboard and monthly KPI view; a two-page Quick Start PDF; and a Customer Follow-Up & AI Vault PDF with message templates and editable prompts.

**Who it's for:** Owner-operated plumbing, HVAC, electrical, cleaning, landscaping, painting, handyman and other home-service businesses.

**How to use:** Enter your costs and assumptions in yellow cells, build a quote, copy final quote values to your job records, enter actual costs after the job, then review the dashboard.

**Requirements:** Microsoft Excel capable of opening .xlsx. No macros. Other spreadsheet apps have not been compatibility-tested. This is not a hosted service or mobile app.

**Disclosures:** Preview job numbers are illustrative and not included as customer data. Dashboard revenue reflects entered completed-job billed amounts, not cash collected. Target margin after allocated overhead differs from accounting gross margin. The toolkit is an operational planning template, not accounting, tax, legal or financial advice. Validate all costs, pricing, tax and permit requirements. Digital download only; no physical item.
