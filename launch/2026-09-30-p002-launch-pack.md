# ULTRON Launch Pack — 2026-09-30

## Product 002 — Plumbing Business Operations Kit

**Launch test price:** $24

**Storefront title:** Plumbing Business Operations Kit | Estimate, Job Costing, Pipeline & KPI Tracker

**Short description:** A practical Excel operating system for owner-operators and small plumbing teams to quote jobs, protect margin, track leads and work, compare estimated vs actual job costs, manage maintenance recall, and review operating KPIs.

### Customer outcome
Replace scattered notes and disconnected spreadsheets with one lightweight operating workbook for pricing, pipeline visibility, job profitability, and follow-up.

### Included
- Pricing setup
- Quote builder
- Lead & job pipeline
- Estimated-vs-actual job costing
- Maintenance recall tracker
- KPI dashboard
- Quick Start Guide PDF

### Benefit bullets
- Build quotes from labor, materials, equipment, permits, and other direct costs.
- Compare quoted price with a target-margin price before sending an estimate.
- Track leads and jobs in one operating queue.
- Capture actual completed-job costs to improve future pricing.
- Keep maintenance and recall opportunities visible.
- Review core owner KPIs in a simple dashboard.

### Positioning / disclaimer
Designed for owner-operators and small plumbing teams. Operational planning template only; not accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice. Users should customize labor, material, permit, tax, warranty, and pricing assumptions for their own business.
