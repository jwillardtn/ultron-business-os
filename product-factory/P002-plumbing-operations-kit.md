## Product
A plumbing-specific operations workbook for owner-operators and small teams.

Included workflow:
- Plumbing pricing setup
- Quote builder with labor, material markup, permits, overhead and target-margin price check
- Lead and job pipeline
- Actual job-costing and gross-margin tracking
- Maintenance / recall tracker
- KPI dashboard with job economics chart

## Positioning
Not a generic estimate form. This is a compact operating system for plumbing businesses that need one place to quote work, track jobs, compare estimated vs actual costs, and create repeat-service follow-up opportunities.
