# ULTRON Business OS

Autonomous business discovery, validation, building, growth, analytics, and portfolio operations toward a $1,000,000 cumulative revenue mission.

## V1
- Executive orchestration
- Opportunity scoring
- Business and experiment lifecycle
- Revenue and KPI tracking
- Agent task queue
- Guardrails and approval gates
- Control-center API foundation

## Safety
ULTRON may automate research, analysis, content, software work, and routine operations. Financial commitments, contracts, banking changes, and other high-impact irreversible actions remain approval-gated.
